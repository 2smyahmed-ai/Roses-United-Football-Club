01 — Club

Grown in the City of Roses.

Pasadena is the City of Roses. Roses United is the youth club that wears it on the chest.

Who we are

We are Roses United.

Roses United FC is a youth football club in Pasadena, California, for boys and girls. We field club teams, run small-group sessions, and coach players one-on-one.

The approach is simple and the parents describe it better than we can: patient coaching, real encouragement, and a team where every kid matters. Players learn skills, but they also learn how to win, lose and support each other like teammates.

You will find us training around Pasadena: at Brookside Park by the Rose Bowl, at Villa Parke, and on turf on weeknights. Exact times and fields go to families when you book.
