ROSES UNITED FC - DEMO WEBSITE
Built by Trace Studio

HOW TO OPEN
Double-click index.html. It opens in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
Keep the "assets", "img" and "video" folders next to index.html.
Needs an internet connection for the fonts.
To share a link instead, upload the whole folder to any static host (Vercel, Netlify).

PAGES
Home, Club, Teams, Training, Match Centre, Gallery, Join.
Things to show on the call:
- EN / ES switch (top right). The whole site is in Spanish too.
- "A session, drawn": the animated pitch. Tap the four phases.
- The three shirt cards flip (hover on desktop, tap on phone).
- The dressing room: tap a shirt number to see that player's photo.
- Match Centre: live countdown, "Add to calendar" downloads a real calendar file.
- Find your age group: pick a birth year.
- Join form: 3 steps, checks the phone and email, then shows a confirmation.
- Gallery: filters, full-screen viewer, both reels play.
- On a phone: bottom bar with "Free first session" and "Next match".
